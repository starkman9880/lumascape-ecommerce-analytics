
# LumaScape | E-Commerce Executive Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview

LumaScape is an end-to-end e-commerce business intelligence portfolio project using Google BigQuery, SQL, and Looker Studio (Data Studio).

The dashboard analyzes e-commerce performance across 2024–2026, focusing on revenue, gross profit, orders, units sold, product categories, and year-over-year growth.

## Technology Stack

- Google Cloud BigQuery — cloud data warehouse
- SQL — data transformation and KPI calculations
- Looker Studio — interactive business intelligence dashboards
- GitHub — documentation and version control

## Dashboard Architecture

E-Commerce Source Data
→ BigQuery Tables
→ SQL Reporting Views
→ Looker Studio
→ Executive Insights and Recommendations

## Page 1 — Executive Performance Overview

**Purpose:** Provide management with a high-level view of business performance.

### Key Performance Indicators

- Net Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Total Orders
- Units Sold
- Gross Margin %
- Revenue Growth YoY %
- Profit Growth YoY %

### Visualizations

- Monthly Net Revenue Trend
- Monthly Gross Profit Trend
- Executive Business Insights

### Business Questions

1. How much revenue and gross profit is the business generating?
2. How are sales and profitability changing over time?
3. How does current-year performance compare with the prior year?

## Page 2 — Sales & Product Performance

**Purpose:** Identify sales trends and the product categories contributing most to business performance.

### Visualizations

- Monthly Total Orders Trend
- Monthly Units Sold Trend
- Net Revenue by Product Category
- Gross Profit by Product Category
- Product Category Insights

### Business Questions

1. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
2. Which categories contribute the most gross profit?
3. How are orders and units sold changing?
4. Which categories warrant additional management attention?

## Page 3 — Profitability & Margin Analysis

**Purpose:** Evaluate profitability growth and gross margin stability.

### Visualizations

- Monthly Gross Profit Trend
- Gross Margin %
- Profitability and Margin Insights

### Business Questions

1. Is gross profit increasing?
2. Is gross margin stable as revenue grows?
3. Is profitability growth being driven by higher sales volume or changes in margin?

## Reporting Period

January 2024 through the available 2026 reporting data.

Dashboard date controls allow users to examine individual periods.

## Key Business Findings

- Transformers are the leading product category by net revenue and gross profit across the full reporting period.
- Monthly sales and profitability increased substantially over the analysis period.
- Gross margin generally remained around 45%–46%.
- Product categories differ in their contributions to revenue and gross profit.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the drivers of Transformer category growth.
2. Compare gross margins across product categories.
3. Monitor sales volume alongside gross profit.
4. Investigate lower-performing product lines.
5. Use monthly and annual reporting to identify emerging changes in performance.
